Welcome

Traditional character, modern comfort

Santorini Mesotopos Suites combines the fresh Aegean breeze with a traditional yet modern way of life, right at the entrance to Fira, the capital of Santorini.

Behind the domes of the suites the sea meets the sky on the horizon. The name Mesotopos means “the place in the middle”: from here the port and the airport are only 10 minutes away, and the whole island is within easy reach.

Before setting out to explore, take a dip in the pool or relax in the jacuzzi in front of the view. It's the perfect start to every day of your holiday.
